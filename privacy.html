🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Urban Hair collects information necessary for providing appointment booking services. This includes display name, email address, phone number, and booking details provided during authentication via Google Sign-In or Phone OTP.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to:

  • Authenticate your user account and verify identity.
  • Facilitate appointment bookings between customers and registered salon owners.
  • Send live booking status updates (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Completed).

3. Data Protection & Security

We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore with strict Role-Based Security Rules. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with unauthorized third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@urbanhair.app.
